# Budget2Go logo concept samples

Created with the built-in image generation tool for brainstorming. The PNGs preserve their original alpha transparency. These samples are not installed into the app.

## Prompt set

Shared direction: minimalist flat vector-like logo for a friendly personal budgeting app, indigo #4F46E5, softly rounded forms, one large symbol with the exact wordmark Budget2Go below. Transparent background and transparent interior negative space, no background tile, shadows, gradients, or watermark.

- **Go Pocket:** thick pocket outline with rounded lower corners; the upper-right edge becomes an integrated forward arrow. Final refinement requested preserving the silhouette and wordmark while removing glow and haze, with solid indigo artwork and fully transparent space outside the artwork and inside the pocket and letter counters.
- **The Clever 2:** bold rounded numeral 2 made from one continuous ribbon, with its horizontal base ending in a compact forward arrow.
- **Pocket B:** uppercase B monogram resembling an upright wallet, with transparent counters and one small clasp tab at the middle right.
